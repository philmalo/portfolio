## Who am I?

I am a full stack web developer, mostly working with PHP and Symfony, Nuxt and
WordPress. I enjoy shipping a feature end to end as much as improving the
tooling behind it, like Docker environments, build pipelines and team
documentation. I favour native solutions and as few dependencies as possible,
because that is what stays maintainable years later.

</section>

<section>

## My background

I ran the operations of a dental prosthetics lab for seventeen years before
switching careers entirely to web development. I completed a web design and
programming diploma at Collège de Maisonneuve, followed by two internships in
2023.

In May 2024, I joined the web team at APCHQ as a front-end integrator. I
gradually took on part of the back-end work, which led to my promotion to full
stack developer in January 2026.

My full background is detailed in [my resume](/cv_en.pdf).

</section>
