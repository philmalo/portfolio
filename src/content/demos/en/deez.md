A mobile-first wine cellar management app, built as a team with [Louis
Roby](https://www.linkedin.com/in/louis-roby-619899a5/) and [Émile
Daigneault](https://www.linkedin.com/in/%C3%A9mile-daigneault-224225252/) as the
capstone of my web development program at Collège de Maisonneuve.

Users manage several cellars, bottle quantities, tasting notes and a shopping
list. The catalogue is imported from the SAQ website by a scraper (Guzzle and
DomCrawler), then indexed with Laravel Scout for search. The app is bilingual
and includes an admin panel.
